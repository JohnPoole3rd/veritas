Known issues:
- Source is the public Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A COG mirror on AWS (Earth Search), not the Copernicus Browser GUI. Same product, and fully scripted.
- Water tiles straddle the western boundary, and some tiles overlap by up to 25 %. The city fits only ~61 disjoint 640 m tiles. See plan/learnings/T10-upper-arlington-tile-budget-and-water.md.
- Road tiles: arterials are 5–11 % of pixels, so they are labelled Highway by EuroSAT convention (the road is the centred, salient feature).
- Forest tiles are the most tree-dense in the city (85–89 % WorldCover tree) but still contain houses under canopy.
- Selection used ESA WorldCover 2021 and OSM only to *choose* locations. Labels are assigned visually in T11.
- Dependencies (add to J's requirements.txt): rasterio, shapely, pyproj, pystac-client, requests, pandas, pillow.
