## Upper Arlington, 50 tiles (R011/R012)

| Method | Acc (all 50) | Acc (37 non-ambiguous) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Procrustes (DINOv2 → rotation → MiniLM captions) | **0.64** | 0.81 |
| ResNet-18 (T08) | **0.64** | 0.81 |
| Local majority (always Residential) | 0.62 | 0.78 |

| Local category (N=10 each) | Procrustes | ResNet-18 | Majority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| residential | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| commercial/industrial | 0.9 | 1.0 | 0.7 |
| road/highway | 0.9 | 0.8 | 0.9 |
| park/forest/herbaceous | 0.4 | 0.3 | 0.5 |
| water/river | **0.0** | 0.1 | 0.0 |

**Bottom line:** out of distribution, both models are only 1/50 better than a constant "Residential" guess. The local set is 62 % Residential, so most of that accuracy is easy. On the hard tiles (water, golf turf, canopy), both fail.

Selected examples (for D/T14 and T13):
- **Success:** ua_011 (Kingsdale-type big-box retail). Procrustes Industrial (score 0.557); ResNet Industrial (0.86).
- **Failure (Procrustes):** ua_049 / ua_041, the Scioto/Griggs channel, predicted **Highway**. A thin linear feature at 10 m looks like a road. 6/10 water tiles → Highway.
- **Failure (ResNet):** ua_036–ua_038, golf-course turf, predicted **Forest with p ≈ 0.9999**: confident and wrong. (Procrustes says Pasture on 2/3, which is arguably right.)

Confidence does not help locally: the mean Procrustes top score is 0.377 when correct vs 0.371 when wrong. ResNet averages p = 0.82 even when wrong. A score threshold would not flag the errors.

## EuroSAT test: per-image comparison (A's T07 review; same full-split artifacts)

| Method | Acc | Macro-F1 | N images |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ResNet-18 (T08) | 0.9817 | 0.9812 | 4050 |
| **Procrustes, image→class** | **0.8121** | 0.7948 | 4050 |

| Unaligned cosine | 0.0827 | 0.0720 | 4050 |

Val is the same (Procrustes 0.8114), so nothing was tuned on test (agg = max, fixed in advance).
Weakest Procrustes classes: River 0.46, Pasture 0.59, PermanentCrop 0.60, Highway 0.78.
J's text→image metric on the same full-split artifacts is top-1 0.96 / top-5 1.00 over 50 caption queries (random 0.18, unaligned 0.10). It measures search, not per-image accuracy. See plan/learnings/general-procrustes-vs-resnet-metrics.md.
